# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the following application:

- **App name**: MÖLKKY.
- **Package name (Application ID)**: com.kanbe1365.molkky
- **Distribution platform**: Google Play Store
- **Developer**: Yuya Kanbetsuawa

Yuya Kanbetsuawa (the "Developer"), the developer of MÖLKKY. (the "App"), respects the privacy of the App's users. This policy explains what data the App handles and how it is treated.

## 1. Collection of Personal Information by the Developer

**The Developer does not collect or store any personal information through the App.**

Data that you create or enter in the App (such as player names, scores, match history, and app settings) is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to the Developer or any third party by the Developer.

## 2. How Data Is Stored

Data created in the App is stored only on your device (local storage) using the Android Room database and DataStore. It is not automatically synced or uploaded to any server operated by the Developer.

## 3. Advertising

The App uses **Google AdMob**, Google's advertising service, to display interstitial ads for monetization.

To deliver and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use information such as your advertising identifier (Advertising ID), IP address, device information, and app usage. This information is handled by Google and may be used to display personalized ads (ads based on your interests).

For details on how Google handles data, please see:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- About AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

## 4. Consent for Advertising (Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App displays a GDPR consent form at launch through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can choose to accept or decline personalized advertising.

You can change your consent settings at any time from "Privacy Settings" on the App's "Settings" screen. (This option is shown only to users in the applicable regions.)

## 5. Opting Out of the Advertising ID

Android users can reset their advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads from their device settings under "Settings > Privacy > Ads" (the exact name varies by device).

## 6. Use of Google Play Services

The App uses Google Play services (including Google Play In-App Review) to provide advertising and in-app review features. These features are provided by Google and are handled in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

- Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 7. What the Developer Does Not Use

To avoid any confusion, the App does **not** use the following:

- Analytics tools (such as Firebase Analytics or Google Analytics)
- Crash reporting tools (such as Firebase Crashlytics)
- Location data
- The Developer's own account registration or login
- Transmission of user data to any server operated by the Developer

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 9. Deletion of Data

When you delete (uninstall) the App, all data stored on your device is also deleted.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the App's features change. If there are significant changes, we will notify you through app updates and by updating this page.
